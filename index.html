The approach

The NextSelf Model.

Leadership development begins with understanding how you currently operate — before deciding how you need to change. Five movements, run in order, revisited as often as the work requires.

01

Understand

What is happening? Name the situation clearly, without flinching from it.

02

Reflect

What role am I playing? Own the pattern, not just the pressure.

03

Develop

What needs to change? Identify the real shift required.

04

Apply

How does it show up? Turn the insight into daily practice.

05

Evolve

Operating at the next level — and knowing why it holds.
