The coach

Indrasanan Krishnan.

Senior executive leader turned coach. Thirty years spent navigating the gap between who we are and who the work needs us to become.

Certified coach Certified mentor 30+ years leadership 5 industries · 4 continents
30+Years real-world experience
20+Professionals coached

We live in a world changing faster than most of us can absorb — and the gap between who we are today and who we need to become is widening by the year. I built my career navigating exactly that gap. Now I help others do the same.

I didn't start at a corner office. I started at the ground floor, and worked my way up over thirty years through five distinct industries: utilities, healthcare, industrial systems, automotive mobility, and Silicon Valley SaaS. That journey took me across four continents and into cultural contexts most professionals never encounter in a single career. I led and scaled global teams of 50 to 150 people, drove regulated mission-critical rollouts, and operated at board and C-suite level.

Here is what I have seen up close: the titles and specialties we built careers around are fading fast. A skill that took years to earn can be disrupted overnight. And yet most of us are still running the same playbook, hoping it holds. The hamster wheel feels productive — but you know the feeling: I can be more. Why can't I get there?

There are millions of frameworks, tools, and techniques available for free. AI can build you a personal development plan in minutes. What it cannot do is sit in the room with you at a real crossroads — when the decision is hard, the stakes are personal, and what you need is not another framework but a thinking partner who genuinely knows your story. That human in the loop is what I am here to be.

My coaching practice did not begin with a certification. I served as visiting faculty coaching young managers on organisational theory, team dynamics, and digital transformation. Informally, over twenty years, I have coached more than twenty professionals — mid-career to senior — through career crises, role transitions, leadership crossroads, and decisions that felt impossible to make alone.

"I strongly believe every individual can build a next version of themselves — without losing the core of who they are. Finding that person, and working alongside them as they become it, is what excites me. It is a journey for me too." — Indrasanan Krishnan

This is not therapy to heal what is hurting. It is a partnership — where the roles we play are different, the commitment is real, and together, in good faith, we build what comes next. I work with my own coach and therapist. I know firsthand what it means to have a skilled human in your corner at a critical moment. That experience is precisely why I keep my roster small.
